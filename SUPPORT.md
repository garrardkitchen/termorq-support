# Getting help with Termorq

- **Read the docs first:** [https://termorq-docs.garrardkitchen.com/](https://termorq-docs.garrardkitchen.com/)
- **Search** [existing issues](https://github.com/garrardkitchen/termorq-support/issues?q=is%3Aissue) — your question may already be answered.
- **Open an issue** with the right template:
  - **Bug report** — something is broken or behaves unexpectedly.
  - **Idea (feature request)** — a new feature or improvement.

## What to expect

- **Pro (paid) and active Trial users** get support priority, including help with bugs, how-to questions, and licence or purchase problems.
- **Free users** are welcome to report bugs and suggest features. These are read and triaged, but Pro support comes first.
- **AI content concerns** — use **Report an AI issue** in the app; it goes to email.

## Keep it safe

- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Never post secrets** — API keys, tokens, passwords, connection strings, private prompts, or personal data. Redact logs and screenshots first.
