# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Termorq Support community to be welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment. Everyone who participates here agrees to act professionally and consider the impact of their words and actions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive when discussing bugs, ideas, and mistakes.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give and receive feedback with care; focus criticism on the product and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or communications without permission.
- Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) when reporting vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualised language or attention, doxxing, and persistent disruption are not acceptable. This also applies to conduct that a reasonable person would find intimidating or abusive in this community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in this repository's issues, discussions, and pull requests, and in other spaces when someone is representing Termorq Support.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code, and may remove content, close or lock issues and discussions, or restrict participation in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They will take account of the circumstances and aim to act fairly and promptly.

To report a conduct concern privately, contact the maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/garrardkitchen). Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
